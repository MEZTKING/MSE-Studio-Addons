# Card prices

What the real cards of your deck – or a saved decklist – cost, on a **Card
prices** page under Review.

- Prices at **TCGplayer** (US dollars, market price) and **Cardmarket** (euros,
  trend price), for the **cheapest printing** of each card or the usual one,
  normal or foil.
- Totals for **everything at TCGplayer**, **everything at Cardmarket**, the
  **cheapest mix** (each card where it costs least), and which store is cheaper
  for the cards both have.
- Shown in your currency (US dollar, euro, pound, Australian, Canadian and New
  Zealand dollar, yen, Swedish krona, złoty).
- **Buy at TCGplayer…** opens TCGplayer's Mass Entry with every card filled in;
  **Copy for Cardmarket** copies a list to paste into a Cardmarket Wants list;
  **Save as CSV…** for a spreadsheet. Double-click a card to open it at a store.

With the Card database add-on (made from *Default cards*, every printing) the
prices come straight from it; otherwise from Scryfall, a couple of cards a
second.

Needs MSE Studio 0.13.0.

## Network

Only when you press Get prices: Scryfall (card prices) and frankfurter.app
(exchange rates, kept for 12 hours). Only card names are sent.
