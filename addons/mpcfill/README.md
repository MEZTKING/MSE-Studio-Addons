# MPCFill

[mpcfill.com](https://mpcfill.com) right inside MSE Studio, on an **MPCFill** page
under Produce.

It opens on MPCFill's **Explore** page (**Explore** and **Editor** buttons
switch) and stays on MPCFill – links to other sites aren't followed.

**Pictures you download** there (*Download Image* on a card) show up beside it
under **Downloaded pictures**, like a decklist's or a proxy deck's cards.
Ctrl/Shift+click to pick several, then (or right-click):

- **Add to my order** – real cards printed with that picture (the Order page).
- **Add to my deck to edit** – your own cards on the Cards page with the
  card's text (from Scryfall) and the picture's art, in your frame or one like
  the real card.
- *Keep only the art…* (the Art only add-on), see it full size, save it, or
  remove it from the list (Delete).

**A whole order:**

1. On the Editor: **Import** your decklist (or search for cards) and pick the
   picture you like for each card.
2. Press **Download → XML** on MPCFill. The order comes straight to the
   **Order (XML)** tab: every card, how many of each, double-faced cards'
   backs and the card back.
3. **Add them to my deck** – they become real cards of your deck, printed with
   the pictures you picked (the Order page and Print at home). **Use its card
   back** makes the order's card back your deck's back.

An order file you saved earlier: **Open an order file…**. The last order is still
there next time.

Pictures come from MPCFill's picture server when it's set (**Picture server…**,
faster and sharper), else straight from the Google Drive they're on. When an
order has two pictures of the same card (different basic land art, say), the
first one is used for all its copies.

Needs MSE Studio 0.17.0 (staying on MPCFill needs 0.18.0). Before 2.0 this
page searched MPCFill itself; the website does that better.

## Network

mpcfill.com, only while the page is open (it's loaded the first time you open
the page), the pictures of an order when you add it, and Scryfall for the text
of cards you add to edit. Nothing about your deck is sent. Orders and
downloaded pictures are kept in the add-on's data folder.
