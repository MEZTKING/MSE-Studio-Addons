# Picture grabber

Pictures for card art from anywhere, on a **Pictures** page under Create:

- **From a web page** – paste a link (Pinterest, ArtStation, DeviantArt, a
  booru, Reddit, MTGPics, any site) and **Find pictures**: every picture on the
  page, the biggest version of each, without logos and icons. Tick and **Save
  ticked**; where each came from is kept for the credits line.
- **Live mode** – every picture you copy and every new screenshot (Win+PrtSc,
  or any folder you add) is saved into the deck's art folder by itself.
- Drag pictures or links onto the page.

Until MSE Studio 0.14 this page came with the app; from 0.15 it's here in the
store. Your settings are kept.

## Network

Only the pages you paste, and the pictures on them.
