# Lore

A page for your deck's world, in **Create → Lore**:

- **The world** – where and when the cards happen, what's going on, the mood
  (*From the plan* brings in what the deck is about, from the Guide).
- **Characters, places, groups and peoples, items, events** and anything else –
  each with a name, other names it goes by, what it is and its story. Events
  have a *When* and are sorted by it.
- **Cards about it** – every card that names it (in its name, type, rules or
  flavour text); double-click one to open it. Its quotes from flavour text, and
  which other entries it mentions or is mentioned by.
- **From my cards…** – what your cards already have: legendary creatures,
  lands and artifacts, sagas, who speaks in flavour text (“…” — Name), the
  types many cards share, and characters saved with the deck.
- **A series' characters…** – a series' whole cast, looked up online (AniList
  for anime and manga, else the series' fan wiki). **Look up its story** adds
  what the fan wiki, AniList or Wikipedia say about one of them.
- **A new card for it**, **Abilities from its story…** (MSE Studio's own
  window, with what's written here) and **Cards for the characters…** – a card
  for each ticked character, with the card type, colours and abilities their
  story fits.
- Below: who speaks in flavour text but isn't in the lore yet, and what has
  no card yet. **Save as a text file…** (Markdown) or **Copy**.

Everything is kept with the deck as `lore.json` in its folder, so it goes with
the deck and its backups.

Needs MSE Studio 0.25.0 or newer. Looking things up online happens only when you
press the buttons that say so.
