# Video capture

Screenshots of episodes with a hotkey (F8, or any key you like), saved straight
into the right episode folder – even while the video is full screen. Pick the
episode, play it, press the key. *Monitor*: "where the mouse is" captures the
screen your mouse pointer is on, or pick one. Your own website links, and
*Add my own images…* / drag & drop.

Until MSE Studio 0.14 this page came with the app; from 0.15 it's here in the
store, so it's only there for people who want it. Your settings are kept.

## Network

None of its own – it opens the links you add in your browser.
