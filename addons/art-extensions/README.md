# Art extensions

One picture across several cards: the art carries on from one card to the
next when they lie side by side – a scene split over a card and its art
cards, a dragon too big for one card, or a row of lands that makes one
landscape.

## How to use it

1. Open the card whose art carries on, on the Cards page.
2. On the **Extend art** page, press **New, from the open card**. The whole
   picture its art was cut from is used (or **Choose…** another one).
3. Add the cards the art carries on to:
   - **Add the open card** / **Add a card…** – cards already in the deck
     (lands, other cards, art cards);
   - **Make an extension card** – a new art card of the first card (never
     played, not counted), in the frame you pick under *New ones' frame*
     and, if you like, without rules text.
4. Put them in order (left to right, then the next row) and set how they
   lie: cards a row, the gap you'll leave between them, zoom and where they
   sit on the picture. The preview shows each card's outline and the part
   of the picture its art box gets.
   - *As if the cards lie side by side*: the frames are windows onto one
     picture – the art lines up across the borders.
   - *Art box to art box*: each art box carries straight on from the one
     before – none of the picture is hidden behind the frames.
5. Press **Put the art on the cards**. Each card gets its part of the
   picture as its art (Undo takes it back). Edit art still works on each
   card afterwards.

A full-art or borderless frame shows the most of the picture. Big pictures
print sharpest: upscale the picture first if the page says the art is
small.

## Where it's kept

The deck's art extensions are kept in `art_extensions.json` in the deck's
folder, and their pictures in its `Art extensions` folder – they go with the
deck and its backups.

Needs MSE Studio 0.25.0 or newer.
