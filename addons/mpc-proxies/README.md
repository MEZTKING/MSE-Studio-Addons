# Proxy decks

Decks people share on [r/mpcproxies](https://www.reddit.com/r/mpcproxies/), Ko-fi
and Discord – real Magic cards with new art and frames – from their public
Google Drive folders, and proxy folders or zips you downloaded to your PC.

The decks are grouped:

- **Free** – import them straight away.
- **Paid – on Ko-fi** – **Buy on Ko-fi** opens the maker's page. After buying,
  Ko-fi gives you a Google Drive link: **I bought it – add my download link…**
  and the deck opens here like the free ones. The link stays on your computer.
- **Added by you** – **Add a link or post…**: paste a Google Drive folder, a
  Reddit post or a Ko-fi page, or a whole post copied from Reddit or Discord.
  **Find the details** reads the deck's name, its maker and its decklist for
  you (untick *Find the name and maker online* to type them yourself).
  **Find on r/mpcproxies…** searches the subreddit; when Reddit won't answer,
  **Search in my browser** and paste a post's link instead.
- **On this PC** – **Add from this PC**: a deck's folder, a folder of decks
  (however deeply they're nested – each deck folder or zip inside is a deck;
  Google Drive's download zips like
  `Monster Hunter Proxies-20250809T134712Z-1-001.zip` are named for you, and
  the parts of one download go together), or zips (pick several at once). Add
  as many folders as you like. Nothing is extracted. **Who made it…** adds the
  maker for the credit.

**Import the deck** reads its cards (names come from the pictures' file names –
`Anzrag, the Quake-Mole (Diablos, Tyrant of the Desert) (1).png` is Anzrag; when
the folder has a decklist, its counts and commander are used). An imported
deck stays imported – it's there when you come back, even after restarting.

Then:

- **Tick** the cards you want with a click – the big tick box is at each card's
  top-left (Ctrl+click works the same). Drag across cards to tick several,
  Shift+click to tick everything up to a card, Ctrl+A for all of them, or
  **Tick all** / **Untick all**. All of the deck's cards start ticked; tokens
  and extras don't.
- Find a card by name, show one **type** (creatures, lands…) or **No rules text
  (often full art)** – basic lands, vanilla creatures and pictures named full
  art or textless – sort by name, folder or copies, and make the cards as big
  as you like (**Size**). **Bigger view** hides the list of decks for more room.
- **Look the cards up on Scryfall** (on by default) gives their types, which
  have rules text, both faces of double-faced cards and the card details in
  your deck. Untick it for custom cards Scryfall doesn't know – nothing is
  looked up then. (With the Card database add-on installed, cards are looked
  up there first.)
- **Add the ticked to my order** – real cards, straight to the Order page:
  printed with these pictures as they are (and in Print at home). Pictures
  without MPC's bleed get it added; double-faced cards get their back face,
  even when it's a separate picture.
- **Add the ticked to my deck to edit** – your own cards on the Cards page,
  with the card's text (from Scryfall) and the picture's art (all of it in the
  art box, like MSE's Force to fit): **in this deck's frame**, in a frame like
  the real card, or in your deck's frame.
- Right-click a card for the same, plus **See it full size** (its back and
  other pictures with ◀ ▶), **Use its art on the card I'm editing**, **Keep
  only the art…** (with the Art only add-on: the art without the frame and
  text), **Print it with…** when there are several pictures, and **Save its
  picture**.
- **Save as a decklist**, **Save the pictures…**, **Use its card back**,
  **Copy the credit**.

### A deck's own frame

Makers often sell a deck's frame templates too (a deluxe edition – the
blank frames, often in a *Templates* folder, or Photoshop files). Right-click
the deck → **This deck's frame…**: pick an MSE frame, or **Make one from the
deck's templates with Frame studio** (an add-on that turns them into an MSE
frame). Then *Add to my deck to edit* puts the deck's cards in it. Templates
aren't shown as cards.

### The list of decks

Right-click a deck: import it, open its folder or Google Drive, who made it,
its frame, save it as a decklist – or **Remove from my list** (the Delete key
does the same; the files on your PC stay). **Bring back removed decks** shows
them again. Decks on this PC can also be taken off a whole folder at a time.

### In my deck

The **In my deck** tab lists the cards of your open deck that came from proxy
decks, by deck – real cards (Order page) and cards to edit (Cards page).
Double-click one to go to it; Delete takes it out of your deck (Undo brings it
back).

Needs MSE Studio 0.17.0.

## Discord

Discord servers (like Magic Proxies) can't be searched by an app without
logging in as you, which breaks Discord's rules – so there's no Discord search.
Copy a post there (its text, links and decklist) and paste the whole thing into
**Add a link or post…**.

## Credits

Every deck belongs to the person who made it. Its credit – the deck, who made
it and where it's from (the Ko-fi page or Reddit post) – is shown with the
deck, kept with every card printed from it (the Order page shows it) and can
be copied. Please support the makers on their Ko-fi pages, and keep their
pictures for your own printing.

## Network

Only when you ask:

- **Google Drive** – a deck's folder list when you import it; its thumbnails
  when you look at it (kept after the first time); its pictures when you see a
  card full size or use them.
- **Reddit** and **Ko-fi** – only when you search r/mpcproxies or read a link you
  pasted.
- **Scryfall** – while *Look the cards up on Scryfall* is ticked: to look a
  deck's cards up after importing it, and when you add them to your deck (as the
  Decklists page does).

Decks on this PC are read from the folders and zips you picked. Nothing about
your deck is sent. Your own decks, links and imported decks are kept in the
add-on's data folder.
