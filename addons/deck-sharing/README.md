# Deck sharing

Share decks with your friends, on a **Deck sharing** page under Manage.

## Through a shared folder

1. Make a folder on OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox and share it with your
   friends there (each of them adds it to their own drive, so it's on their PC).
2. On the page, type **your name** and **Choose…** that folder (*In my cloud
   folders* starts in the ones MSE Studio finds).
3. Open a deck and press **Share this deck**. Add a note if you like. Tick
   **With its card pictures** so friends can print straight away (a much bigger
   file), or **With its art folder** so they can re-crop the art.

Everyone's shared decks are listed with a picture of the commander, who shared
it, when and how many cards. **Open this deck** adds it to your decks folder and
opens it – if you already have a deck with that name, the copy gets a new name
("… (from Sam)"), so nothing of yours is ever replaced. Sharing a deck again
updates your earlier one; **Stop sharing it** takes one of yours out of the
folder (friends who opened it keep their copy).

## As a file

**Send as a file…** saves the open deck as one `.msedeck` file to send on
Discord, by e-mail or on a USB stick. Your friend uses **Open a deck file…**.

## What's in a shared deck

The deck folder (its MSE set, settings, notes, card backs), without your undo
history and the drawn, upscaled and print-ready pictures unless you tick *With
its card pictures*. The art folder only goes in with *With its art folder*.

Needs MSE Studio 0.16.0.

## Network and files

None of its own: OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox keep the shared folder in
step. It reads and writes `.msedeck` files only in the folder you choose (or
where you save one), and unpacks a friend's deck into a new folder in your decks
folder. Your name and the shared folder are kept in the add-on's data folder.
