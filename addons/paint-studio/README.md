# Paint studio

Paint your cards' art in MSE Studio the way you would in a painting app on a
tablet: layers, brushes that feel like real pencils, inks and paint, your
pen's pressure, and the card's frame shown over the painting while you work.
A page under **Create**; MSE Studio's own **Paint this card's art**
(Ctrl+Shift+D) opens it too.

## Starting

- **Paint the art of …** – the card open on the Cards page: carry on with its
  painting, or start one over its art (the shape of its art box, 1600 pixels
  wide). **Use as the card's art** puts the painting on the card – one step in
  the deck's history, so Undo takes it back.
- **New painting…** – a card's art box, a whole borderless card, square,
  wide or your own size, on white, see-through or coloured paper.
- **Open a picture or painting…** – an `.ora` painting (Krita, GIMP and
  MyPaint save these), a Photoshop file with its layers, or any picture.

Paintings are kept with the deck (`paintings`, and a card's painting in
`art-edits/paintings`) and saved every minute and a half while you paint.
Right-click one on the page to put it on a card, export it or delete it.

## Painting

- **34 brushes** in sets – sketching (HB and 6B pencils, technical pen, fine
  liner), inking (studio pen, ink brush, dry ink, marker), painting (round,
  flat, oil, gouache, acrylic, blender), airbrushes, watercolour (wet wash with
  darker wet edges, watercolour round, splatter), textures (chalk, charcoal,
  sponge, stipple, clouds), effects (glow pen, light, sparkles), smudging and
  blur, and erasers. Each shows a stroke of what it does.
- **Brush studio** (Edit brush…) – every setting with a live stroke and a scrap
  of paper to try it on: size and flow with a light touch, spacing, steadier
  lines, taper, scatter, sprays, hardness, a flat tip that turns with the
  stroke, paper grain, colour and size changes along the stroke, wet paint
  that picks up the colour under it, and how it mixes (multiply, add for
  glows…). Your brushes are kept in **My brushes**; **Share…** saves them to a
  file or adds brushes from one.
- **Smudge** and **Erase** use any brush's shape, each remembering its own
  brush, size and opacity – like the paint tool.
- **Hold to straighten** – finish a line and keep the pen still: it becomes a
  straight line, or a neat ellipse when it closes.
- **Mirror** left–right, top–bottom, four ways, or a kaleidoscope of 6, 8 or
  12.
- **Fill** (up to the lines on every layer, with a colour tolerance),
  **gradients** (straight or round), **liquify** (push, twirl, pinch, swell,
  or bring back what was there), **shapes** (rectangles, ellipses, lines –
  filled, or drawn with the brush) and **Pick a colour** (or Alt + click).
- **Selections** – rectangle, ellipse, freehand or the magic wand; add with
  Shift, take away with Alt, soften the edge, make it bigger or smaller, turn
  it inside out, or select a layer's paint. Painting, filling and
  adjustments stay inside it.
- **Move and resize** the layer or the selected part: drag, resize from the
  corners (Shift: freely), turn with the blue dot, flip, or fill the painting.
- **Layers** – as many as you like, with opacity, 13 blend modes (multiply
  for shadows, screen and add for light, overlay…), **Clip to the layer
  below**, **Paint only on its paint**, locks, merge and flatten.
- **14 adjustments** with a live preview – hue, saturation and brightness,
  colour balance, levels, contrast and exposure, blur, motion blur, sharpen,
  noise, glow, gradient map, posterise, vignette, chromatic aberration and
  invert – on the layer or the selection.
- **Colour** – a colour wheel, the colour's values, harmonies (opposite,
  neighbours, triangle…), recent colours, palettes (and one made from the
  painting).
- **The card's frame on top** (Actions → Canvas) while painting a card's art,
  so you see what will show; **See through the frame** lightens it. A thirds
  grid helps with the layout, and a reference picture can sit beside it.

## Pen, touch and keys

A drawing tablet's pressure changes the size and flow; the pen's eraser end
erases. On a touch screen two fingers move, zoom and turn the canvas, a
two-finger tap undoes and a three-finger tap redoes. With a mouse, *thinner
when fast* gives strokes a feel of pressure.

B paint · S smudge · E erase · G fill · D gradient · I pick a colour · M select
· V move · L liquify · U shapes · [ and ] size · 1–9 opacity · X swap colours ·
the wheel zooms · Shift + wheel turns · Space + drag moves · Ctrl+0 fits · R
turns it back · H mirrors the view · Tab hides the panels · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.
Actions → Keyboard and touch help lists them all.

Exports: PNG, JPG, every layer as a PNG, or an `.ora` copy.

Needs MSE Studio 0.25.0.

## Network and files

No network at all. Paintings go in the deck's folder (or, without a deck,
Paint studio's data folder); your brushes, palettes and brush pictures in
Paint studio's data folder; exports where you choose.
